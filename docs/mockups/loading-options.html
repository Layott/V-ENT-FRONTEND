Loading, three ways

Today a page shows a blank dark surface while it loads, so a press looks like nothing happened. Each option below starts the moment a link is pressed. None of them pulses, shimmers or glows: the only motion is the loading itself, and with reduced motion on it stands still. Pick one, or say what to change.

A. A line at the top, the page's shape below

A red line runs under the header while the page loads. Beneath it, the outline of the page that is coming: the banner, the stats, the tabs, the sections. Recommended: people see at once that the press worked and what is on its way.

B. The shape fills in, once

No moving line. The outline of the page arrives block by block, top to bottom, in about half a second, and then holds still until the real page replaces it.
