Honest answers.

Quick read.

What am I joining?

The V-ENT pre-launch list. We save your email plus any optional details you give us (display name, username, country, favorite game, password). On launch we send a one-click claim link to that email. Clicking it activates a full account with everything you reserved already populated.

Do I need to set a password now?

No. Email-only works fine. If you set a password now, claim is one click; if you don't, the launch email contains a magic link. Either way works.

Is my username actually reserved?

Yes. Locked against your email. Nobody else can take it. If you don't claim within 30 days of launch, the username releases back to the pool.

How does "skip the line" work?

Each person who joins via your referral link bumps your position +5. Top 1,000 positions at launch get the founder badge + VC bonus.

How do you handle my data?

We only collect what you give us. Stored encrypted. Used only for launch updates and to provision your account. Email info@v-ent.co any time to delete your record.

When is launch?

Phase 1 (Tournaments + Wallet + Admin) targets Q4 2026. Waitlist members get access first. Later joiners on a rolling weekly basis.
